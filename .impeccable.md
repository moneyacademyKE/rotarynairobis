## Design Context

### Users
Rotary Club of Nairobi South (RCNS) members and the wider community. Using the app to catch up on social activity, celebrate milestones, and discover club news across Twitter, Instagram, and TikTok feeds.

### Brand Personality
Precision-focused (Rich Hickey / Simple Made Easy), Community-centric, and Celebratory.

### Aesthetic Direction
Edge-native High Performance. Editorial layouts with fluid scaling. Adaptive platform themes that respect the source material (Twitter/Instagram/TikTok) while maintaining a high-fidelity "RCNS" visual wrapper.

### Design Principles
1. Performance is the primary delight (zero-latency, 60fps).
2. De-complected identity (Clear separation of platform modes).
3. Visual truth (No placeholders, real media projection).
